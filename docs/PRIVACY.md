# Privacy Policy Draft

This describes the native app and must be reviewed and published with final publisher contact information before release.

## Files

Pliegue reads files you select or drop into the app and saves results to a location you choose. Selecting a folder includes its readable descendants, including hidden files; symbolic links are rejected. Only included assets are processed, and external model references are not collected automatically. Size, modification date, duration, sample format, and resolution are used for compression and verification. Files and metadata are not uploaded. Original inputs are never replaced. Converted photos, audio, and videos may have changed metadata; ZIP preserves file contents and internal folder paths, not every filesystem attribute.

## Temporary data

Results are staged locally and removed on cancellation, failure, clearing the result, or normal shutdown. Force-quitting or crashing can leave temporary files for operating-system cleanup. Explicitly saved results remain until you delete them.

## Accounts, purchases, and analytics

Pliegue has no account registration, advertising, analytics SDK, or separate payment service. The planned purchase is paid once through the Mac App Store. Apple handles purchases, downloads, and platform diagnostics under its policies and your device settings. Pliegue does not receive payment card details.

## Contact

A public support address and responsible publisher details must be added before release. There is no live store listing or customer support service configured in this development project.
